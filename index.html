About

Philosophy

The brief I give every team

“Solve a real problem, simply. Let the research argue, let the humans decide — and ship it better than last time.”
01

Grow people, not just pixels

I build teams that argue well, challenge assumptions, and outgrow their job titles. Promotions are my favourite deliverable.

02

Design is a business tool

Craft that doesn't move a number is a hobby. I align the work to real objectives so nobody has to defend the design team's existence — including me.

03

Humans first, dashboards second

Every decision starts with the people behind the screen — their needs, their frustrations, and the things they'd never write in a survey.

04

Perfect is a direction

Not a destination. Great work comes from testing, learning and refining — and from knowing when version three beats version perfect.
